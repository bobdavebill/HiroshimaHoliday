Activities

Here are some fun things to do in Hiroshima. We have a full 5 day plan

Day Activity Description Image
1 Hiroshima Castle Take a local tour around Hiroshima Castle.
2 Senko-ji Temple Visit Senko-ji Temple to further understand Japanese culture.
3 Itsukushima Take the ferry to Itsukushima Island.
4 Shukkei-en Garden Visit Shukkei-en Garden to take in beautiful views of traditional Japanese Gardens.
5 Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park Experience the history of Hiroshima's sad and tragic story, and learn about how people learned to overcome it.
