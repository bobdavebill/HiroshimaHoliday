Reflections and Bibliography

Here are the reflections and bibliographies from each team member


Reflections


Yi Fei

The most important sustainability decision I made was to choose the Grand Prince Hotel Hiroshima because it uses locally sourced ingredients and also reduces the use of single-use plastics and they also participate in coastal clean-up activities. These activities help to clean up the environment and also help benefit local businesses. One challenge I faced was finding suitable information about the hotel and the way its activities are really sustainable or not. When I first heard about ecotourism, I thought it was about going to places that have nature but now I know that it is about making sustainable decisions when going on holidays and supporting the local community.


Jonny

The most important sustainability decision I made was providing local food and accommodation to the travellers, which keeps it sustainable. I faced challenges when trying to find a graph about Hiroshima, as it didn't come at all when i searched it up, so I compromised and just did Japan statistics.


Kayden

Personally, the most important sustainability decision I made was to elaborate on Hiroshima’s painful past through educating users of the website about the tragic events of WW2 which impacted Hiroshima, elevating and respecting commemorating the victims of the atomic bombing on August 6, 1945. Hence encouraging social sustainability on our website.

Whilst creating and explaining my sustainability impacts, it occurred to me that Hiroshima had an inadequate amount of environmentally contributing activities, which resulted in a prolonged period of research which made me sacrifice copious amounts of precious time.


Anay

The most important decision I made was to elaborate on the environmental sustainability of our activities compared to other popular activities in Japan, as well as talking about transport options, making the trade off to choose public transport to support the environment as compared to taking a taxi to support local drivers and build jobs. My biggest challenge was finding energy usage data from a major theme park to refer to in my sustainability explaination.
