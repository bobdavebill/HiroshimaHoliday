Sustainability

Here is how we consider sustainability accross this holiday


Social

Through left remains by victims, which includes testimonies, artefacts and related materials, the tours of the UNESCO Hiroshima peace memorial buildings, it allows us to demonstrate and educate our visitors about the horrors of World War 2, tutoring our visitors on the tragic events of Hiroshima. Through our tours we can facilitate and raise awareness about the challenges of Hiroshima and their grievous cultural history.
Educate yourself by journeying and meditating in the Shukkeien Garden. Learn about Japan’s cultural practices by indulging yourself in our weekly ceremonies which are regularly hosted inside historic tea houses on the grounds, such as the Seifukan and Meigetsu-tei. By promoting and encouraging our visitors to experience in these practices, we can maintain and ensure societal sustainability to Hiroshima’s culture, history and identity through the continued education of these practices.


Economic

Experience and learn about Hiroshima Castle’s complex history and heritage with our sponsored tour guides. Through your donations, your funds will be used to improve and maintain Hiroshima’s economic condition, allowing your generous funds to be circulated in Hiroshima’s growing economy. Furthermore, this will facilitate both micro and macro aspects of economic expansion, allowing for improved infrastructure, services and in general a better economy for Hiroshima.


Environmental

All of our activities are environmentally sustainably as all activities are at natural places, or at museums reducing the emissions, from these activities. These activities are much more environmentally sustainable than going to a theme park, as these places contribute to noise, air and light pollution. Disneyland in Tokyo spends over $102,000 on electricity everyday. People going on this holiday are strongly encouraged to use public transport to access activities to have less emissions per person as compared to a car or taxi. Busses are very cheap with the average adult bus fare being around $2, similar to what it is here in Australia, making it worthwhile.
