Accommodation

Here is a sustainable eco-friendly place to stay

Grand Prince Hotel Hiroshima supports ecotourism through several activities that reduce environmental impacts and benefit the local community. The hotel has gotten rid of disposable plastic straws, introduced paper yoghurt containers to reduce plastic waste, and also participates in coastal clean-up activities around Motoujina. It also uses fruit from a local orchard that might otherwise be wasted and offers spa treatments using locally sourced Hiroshima lemon oil. The guests can explore nearby natural areas, hire bicycles and travel by boat towards Miyajima. These activities can encourage visitors to appreciate Hiroshima's natural environment while supporting local businesses. The hotel has Sakura Quality Green certification which provides further evidence of its sustainability efforts. The Sakura Quality Green certification is a programme that assesses environmental and social responsibility as well as sustainable management. However, the hotel still consumes energy and water, so continued improvements are necessary to minimise its overall environmental footprint.
